    How to play

    With a mouse

    • Left-click to select; drag to select a group; double-click a soldier to select every one of that kind on screen.
    • Right-click to order: on the ground to move, on trees, gold or berries to gather, on an enemy to attack, on an unfinished building to help build.
    • Move the view with the arrow keys or by pushing the mouse to the edge; scroll to zoom; click the minimap to jump.

    On a touch screen

    • Tap your units to select them, then tap where they should go or what they should work on or attack. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, and press and hold, then drag to select a group. The ⚔️ button selects your whole army.

    Building a city

    • Villagers gather food (berries, then farms), wood and gold, and carry it to the Agora or a Storehouse. Build Storehouses near trees and gold.
    • Every House holds five people. Build them before you run out of room.
    • At the Agora, advance to the Classical Age (archers, cavalry, temples, towers) and the Heroic Age (rams; heroes grow stronger).
    • A Temple slowly earns the favour of the gods and heals units near it. Spend favour on god powers at the top of the screen.

    Fighting

    • Hoplites beat cavalry; cavalry ride down archers and rams; archers wear down hoplites from range. Rams wreck buildings.
    • Heroes deal triple damage to mythical creatures. Some monsters can only be beaten by a hero.

    Keys

    Enter opens the chat box, where cheat codes work (see the Codex) · . idle villager · H Agora · A attack-move · S stop · Ctrl+1–9 make a group, 1–9 select it · Space jump to the last alarm · Esc cancel or pause. The letters on the command buttons are their hotkeys.
